Axial capacity of CFST columns with random overlapping pitting corrosion

Single Calculation

Parameters [fy (MPa), fc (MPa), D0 (mm), t0 (mm), L (mm), α (°), l (mm), h (mm), VLR, Note]

Note defaults to empty; enter "Nokpu" to ignore adverse effect of pitting corrosion.

Batch Calculation

Parameters [fy (MPa), fc (MPa), D0 (mm), t0 (mm), L (mm), α (°), l (mm), h (mm), VLR, Note]

Supported formats: excel, txt, CSV (9 numeric columns + optional 10th column for Note)
fy /MPafc /MPaD0 /mmt0 /mm L /mmα /°l /mmh /mm VLRNoteNu,PC /kN
Note: Adverse effect of pitting corrosion is ignored for specimens with "Nokpu" note.
